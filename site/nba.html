Player props — hit rates vs line

Every player with 3+ games of 15+ minutes. Line is the posted book line when SIGNAL captured one (book), otherwise the player's season baseline: the median of his last 20 eligible games, rounded to .5 (signal-median). An L5 or L10 rate against that baseline is a real recent trend; the cushion is how far his L10 median sits from it. HOT/COLD-STREAK-PRICED means the recent window has run away from the longer sample — that's where the book has usually already moved.
